# The Witcher (2007 / Enhanced Edition) recap research

## What sequence does the recap establish?

### Takeaway
The ten entries follow the fixed main-story spine from Kaer Morhen to the final palace cinematic, while describing variable outcomes explicitly. The content is original paraphrase, not extracted guide text.

### Cited Findings
- Kaer Morhen’s mutagens are stolen and Leo dies regardless of Geralt’s defence assignment. Chapter `w1-kaer-morhen` uses [Defending Kaer Morhen](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/defendingkaermorhen.php) and the [official manual](https://ftpmirror.your.org/pub/misc/ftp.atari.com/manuals/pc/witcher/WitcherManual.pdf), particularly character descriptions and the amnesia explanation on printed page 37.
- `w1-outskirts` combines the [Beast and Abigail quest](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/ofmonstersandmen.php) with [the Salamandra hideout trail](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/thesalamanderstail.php).
- `w1-vizima-investigation` maps to [Prison Break](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/prisonbreak.php), [Vizima Confidential](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/vizimaconfidential.php) and [Anatomy of a Crime](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/anatomyofacrime.php). Azar replaces Raymond; false accusations are possible.
- `w1-trade-quarter` maps to [A Posh Reception](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/aposhreception.php), [Lock and Key](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/lockandkey.php) and [Thaler’s journal](https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Thaler_%28spy%29).
- `w1-source-and-assault` maps to [The Source](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/thesource.php), [Gold Rush](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/goldrush.php) and [The Unforgiven](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/theunforgiven.php).
- `w1-murky-waters` maps to [Alvin](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/alvin.php), [The Heat of the Day](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/theheatoftheday.php) and [Ripples](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/ripples.php).
- `w1-destiny-and-choice` maps to [The Paths of Destiny](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/thepathsofdestiny.php) and [Free Elves](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/freeelves.php).
- `w1-burning-vizima` maps to [Under a Fiery Sky](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/underafierysky.php) and [Her Highness the Striga](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/herhighnessthestriga.php).
- `w1-old-manor` maps to [Sweet Revenge](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/sweetrevenge.php) and the transcribed [Witchers’ Secrets journal](https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Witchers%27_Secrets).
- `w1-frozen-reflections` maps to [The Ashes of Vizima](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/theashesofvizima.php), [Frozen Reflections](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/frozenreflections.php), [Jacques’s stated purpose](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/the-time-of-the-white-frost-and-the-white-light/the-witcher/guide) and [the ending cinematic summary](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Witcher_storyline).

### Inferences
- The prose draws thematic connections between the theft and the resulting experiments, and between the human conflicts and monster work. Those are editorial framing, not claims that developers explained their intentions.

### Gaps
- No complete official first-game plot guide was found on CDPR’s current extras page. The manual is official primary material hosted on an Atari FTP archive mirror; most detailed sequencing is secondary walkthrough evidence. Community-transcribed journal text is clearly labelled.

## How are player decisions preserved?

### Takeaway
There is no invented canonical route. A fixed event is separated from the outcomes a returning player might remember differently.

### Cited Findings
- The bank decision affects the ally for the assault, while the later village decision still permits neutrality. [Gold Rush](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/goldrush.php), [Free Elves](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/freeelves.php).
- Chapter V supports separate [neutral](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/underafierysky.php), [Order](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/theflamethatcleanses.php) and [Scoia’tael](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/hopeburnsbright.php) routes.
- Abigail, Vincent, Berengar and Adda have alternative fates; Siegfried and Yaevinn are not automatically dead on the neutral route. [Abigail](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/ofmonstersandmen.php), [Vincent](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/lockandkey.php), [Berengar](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/thepathsofdestiny.php), [Adda](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/herhighnessthestriga.php), [faction leaders](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/theashesofvizima.php).

### Inferences
- None of the supported options is elevated to the one true playthrough. The recap is intentionally compatible with different saved decisions.

### Gaps
- This is an in-depth main-story recap, not an exhaustive catalogue of contracts, sexual encounters or every minor decision. It does not claim that every first-game flag is imported by the sequels.

## Which claims require special care?

### Takeaway
The first game strongly implies Alvin is Jacques through clues. The ending assassin’s origin must not be invented.

### Cited Findings
- Alvin’s powers, the aged amulet and Jacques’s familiar remarks support the connection; the community reference itself uses qualified wording. [Alvin journal and notes](https://witcher-games.fandom.com/wiki/Alvin_%28quest%29).
- GameBanshee states the identity directly, then speculates that the ending assassin may come from Jacques’s experiments. The recap preserves the identity as the first game’s strong implication and rejects the unsupported assassin speculation. [Frozen Reflections](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher/walkthrough/frozenreflections.php).
- The final cinematic shows an unfamiliar assassin with witcher eyes. It does not name him. [Storyline: ending cinematic](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Witcher_storyline).
- Jacques describes his mutant knights as protection against the White Frost. This is attributed to Jacques, not presented as independent proof his programme was necessary. [Final confrontation](https://www.gamerguides.com/the-witcher/guide/walkthrough/epilogue/the-time-of-the-white-frost-and-the-white-light/the-witcher/guide).

### Inferences
- The recap’s Alvin wording deliberately describes what the first game communicates. It does not treat the connection as a fan theory of equal weight with random alternatives, or disclose later Witcher 3 quest material.

### Gaps
- The GameBanshee Free Elves page incorrectly calls White Rayla an elf. Only her role as an Order commander is used. Other source editorial judgements about the morally correct route, combat advice and speculation are excluded.
- No exact date is assigned to the first game: avoiding unnecessary chronology precision prevents the early game’s dating inconsistencies from being silently resolved.
- Research checked on 19 October 2026. Some Fandom direct opens failed; indexed page text was available and used only where its relevant text was actually returned.
