# The Witcher 2 recap and the transition from The Witcher

## What connects the first two games, and what is safe to say about Geralt’s starting position?

### Takeaway
The narrative connection is direct: Geralt saves Foltest in the first game’s closing scene and is with the king’s army in the second game’s prologue. No exact elapsed time or game calendar year is needed. [CDPR, Marcin Blacha](https://www.thewitcher.com/us/en/news/585/new-video-find-out-what-happened-in-the-first-witcher-game), [Prologue dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/prologue/)

### Cited Findings
- Blacha explicitly describes the sequel as beginning just after the first game; its story moves east from Vizima while retaining Triss, Dandelion and Zoltan. [CDPR statement](https://www.thewitcher.com/us/en/news/585/new-video-find-out-what-happened-in-the-first-witcher-game)
- The first game’s final intruder is not identified in that scene as Letho, Auckes or Serrit. The recap therefore leaves him unnamed. [Game overview and preceding-game connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Witcher_2:_Assassins_of_Kings)
- The prologue reconstructs Foltest’s campaign against the La Valettes; Aryan can surrender or die. The children Anaïs and Boussy are Foltest’s by Louisa La Valette. [Prologue dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/prologue/)
- A disguised monk murders Foltest and escapes while Geralt is left to face the soldiers. Roche subsequently helps Geralt escape to pursue the actual killer. [Monastery sequence](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/theprologuestory4.php), [Dungeon escape](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/thedungeonsofthelavalettes.php)
- Flotsam introduces the surviving Dandelion and Zoltan, Loredo’s ties to Dethmold, and Síle’s partnership in killing the kayran. [Gallows](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/bythegods-stringingupsods.php), [Loredo](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/indecentproposal.php), [Kayran](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/thekayran.php)

### Inferences
- Editorial organisation separates the pre-W1 amnesia mystery from the W1-to-W2 transition. This is a reading aid, not an additional fictional event.

### Gaps
- I deliberately omit an absolute year and an exact count of elapsed days. Those details are unnecessary here and game calendar inconsistencies should not become invented precision.
- Books and side media are covered in `continuity.md`. The bridge entry states that the book saga does not occur between these two games, but does not independently position every comic or spin-off. Cross-check the continuity notes.

## Which route events and choices must remain distinct?

### Takeaway
The second chapter is two alternative campaigns. The sword choice at the elven ruins is also distinct from the later decision to leave with Roche or Iorveth. [Flotsam assassination investigation](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/chapter1theassassinsofkings.php), [Flotsam Triss investigation](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/chapter1whereistrissmerigold.php)

### Cited Findings
- Letho defeats Geralt and spares him because Geralt once saved his life. He forces Triss to teleport him near Vergen; Cedric’s final testimony confirms the departure. [First confrontation](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/chapter1theassassinsofkings.php), [Cedric and the crossroads](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/chapter1whereistrissmerigold.php)
- Choosing Roche entails the Loredo operation; choosing Iorveth entails the prison-barge escape. The paths provide different perspectives rather than successive acts in one journey. [Chapter I dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/chapter-1/)
- Henselt’s killing of Olcan reactivates the battlefield crisis. Henselt’s personal blood curse is not identical to the mist; lifting one does not end the other. [Aedirn prelude](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/preludetowaraedirniorveth.php), [Blood curse](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/thebloodcurseroche.php)
- Geralt relives battlefield roles, confronts the draug and removes the spectral obstacle. His own memory of rescuing Letho also returns around this phase. [Eternal Battle](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/theeternalbattleroche.php)
- Roche’s route includes Auckes and Serrit’s attack, Dethmold’s necromancy and the investigation of their hideout. [Prima Kaedweni camp guide](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-2-assassins-of-kings-eguide/walkthrough/chapter-2-the-kaedweni-camp-roche)
- Henselt orders the Blue Stripes’ execution and rapes Ves. In conquered Vergen, Geralt can stop Roche from killing him. [Chapter II Roche dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/chapter-2-roche/)
- Iorveth’s route has Saskia’s poisoning, Stennis’s lynching-or-trial decision and the alternative of Henselt’s blood. The story has alternatives, so the crowd’s allegation is not recast as a universally established verdict. [Royal Blood](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/royalbloodiorveth.php), [Saskia’s cure](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/amatteroflifeanddeathiorveth.php)
- Saskia reveals her dragon identity during the defence. After Henselt surrenders, investigation reveals that Philippa used the rose to establish magical control. [Vergen Besieged](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/vergenbesiegediorveth.php)
- The compressed figurine is Triss herself, Cynthia is an imperial agent, and Shilard takes Triss to Loc Muinne. Roche and Ves assist Geralt on Iorveth’s version of the investigation. [Triss in Aedirn](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/chapter2whereistrissmerigoldiorveth.php), [Chapter II Iorveth dialogue](https://laurelnose.github.io/chapter-2-iorveth/)
- At Loc Muinne, Roche’s path offers Triss or Anaïs, then Radovid or Natalis for Anaïs; Iorveth’s path offers Triss or Philippa’s means of freeing Saskia. [Prima Roche chapter III](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-2-assassins-of-kings-eguide/walkthrough/chapter-3-loc-muinne-roche), [Prima Iorveth chapter III](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-2-assassins-of-kings-eguide/walkthrough/chapter-3-loc-muinne-iorveth)

### Inferences
- The recap’s advice to read both perspectives is editorial. It does not claim one Geralt experienced both campaigns.

### Gaps
- Do not assign a canonical survival to Aryan, Henselt, Síle, Saskia or Letho. Do not imply that saving Triss also grants the spellbreaker dagger. [Ending alternatives](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Witcher_2_ending)

## What does the ending establish, and where does testimony stop short of proof?

### Takeaway
Separate the Lodge’s actual commission against Demavend from Emhyr’s wider destabilisation programme. Letho gives Geralt essential pre-W1 memories and Yennefer’s last known whereabouts, while explicitly admitting limits to his knowledge. [Chapter III dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/chapter-3-roche/), [Epilogue dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/epilogue/)

### Cited Findings
- The summit’s exposure happens through Triss or through Shilard and Letho depending on the rescue. The treatment of the gathered mages differs between those outcomes. [Summit walkthrough](https://www.gamebanshee.com/thewitcher2/walkthrough/asummitofmagesiorveth.php), [Prima Roche aftermath](https://primagames.com/eguides/the-witcher-2-assassins-of-kings-eguide/walkthrough/chapter-3-loc-muinne-roche)
- Síle acknowledges Demavend’s assassination plot but denies the later regicides. Her portal has been sabotaged, and Geralt can remove the defective diamond or let her die. [Chapter III dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/chapter-3-roche/)
- The special dagger permits freeing Saskia. Without the relevant path Geralt cannot perform the cure. [Official English quest handbook](https://dlc-patcher.thewitcher.com/Bonus/Quest_Handbooks/The_Witcher_2_English_Quest_Handbook/The_Witcher_2_-_Guide_-_134x190.pdf)
- Letho names Emhyr as his patron and the restoration of the Viper school as his promised reward. His mission was to kill rulers, implicate sorceresses and prepare for invasion. Geralt can spare or kill him. [Epilogue dialogue transcription](https://laurelnose.github.io/epilogue/)
- Geralt’s pre-W1 history includes trading himself to the Hunt for Yennefer. Letho subsequently travelled with her and was captured in Nilfgaard. He says she was in the Empire when he left, not that he knows her current precise situation. [Official handbook’s returning-memory material](https://dlc-patcher.thewitcher.com/Bonus/Quest_Handbooks/The_Witcher_2_English_Quest_Handbook/The_Witcher_2_-_Guide_-_134x190.pdf), [Ending reference](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/The_Witcher_2_ending)

### Inferences
- Letho’s belief that Nilfgaard obtained the Lodge’s secrets through Yennefer is explicitly his belief. The recap labels it accordingly; it does not assert she knowingly betrayed the Lodge. [Epilogue dialogue](https://laurelnose.github.io/epilogue/)
- The political/personal handoff to W3 is editorial synthesis of the ending, not a statement of later W3 outcomes.

### Gaps
- The dialogue source is a community transcription, not an official published script. It is labelled that way in all 28 source records; guides are also explicitly labelled secondary.
- The publisher-hosted PDF is indexed with relevant text, but its direct web fetch returned an internal error. I used its indexed text only as a cross-check, not as a claim that every page had been read.
- Fandom’s full ending page was blocked in direct fetch. Its indexed snippets supported cross-checks only. Most detailed claims are independently supported by loaded dialogue pages and published walkthroughs.
- This module deliberately ends at the W2 epilogue. It does not explain later W3 quest resolutions, romances, deaths or war endings.
